## 5. MOTION DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Implement professional animations throughout.

### Camera movements

Use:

- Slow cinematic push-ins
- Gentle camera pullbacks
- Horizontal and vertical pans
- Controlled perspective transformations
- Subtle parallax
- Smooth product focus transitions

Avoid excessive spinning and artificial 3D effects.

### Transitions

Create reusable transitions including:

- Cross dissolves
- Directional slides
- Mask reveals
- Subtle motion-blur transitions
- Zoom-through transitions
- Soft light sweeps

Use transitions intentionally.

Do not apply the same transition to every scene.

### Typography animation

Support:

- Fade and rise
- Word-by-word reveals
- Letter spacing animation
- Smooth masked reveals
- Staggered text entrance
- Elegant headline exits

Avoid exaggerated bouncing typography.

### Motion behavior

Animations must be smooth and deterministic.

Use Remotion's frame-based animation primitives, interpolate, spring, and official transitions where appropriate.

Prefer restrained cinematic easing over unnecessary movement.

Ensure the commercial looks good even when individual frames are paused.
